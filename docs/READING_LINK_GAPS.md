# Reading Link Gaps

Last reviewed: 2026-08-08

The curated 96-week curriculum prefers official government, military, NATO, archival, or public-domain links. The following readings intentionally remain without a direct URL rather than linking to an unverified, unstable, paywalled, or unauthorized copy.

## Copyrighted books — obtain through a library or owned copy

- **Louis P. Masur, _The Civil War: A Concise History_ (Oxford University Press, 2011).** No free official full-text URL is expected; do not add an unauthorized PDF.
- **Edward N. Luttwak, _Strategy: The Logic of War and Peace_, revised ed. (Belknap Press, 2001).** No free official full-text URL is expected; do not add an unauthorized PDF.

## Historical-source selections still needing a stable course link

- **FM 100-5, _Operations_ (1982/1986 AirLand Battle editions).** Army University Press and TRADOC provide official historical discussions of the manuals, but this course still needs a stable official full-manual scan before a direct URL is added. Useful official context: Army University Press, “FM 100-5: The AirLand Battle in 1986.”
- **Soviet Operational Art and Deep Operations.** CIA FOIA contains useful declassified Soviet/Warsaw Pact primary material, including _Soviet Military Power 1985_ and translated operational-training documents, but the course should select one bounded reading before hard-coding a URL.
- **The 1973 Arab-Israeli War.** A stable official public campaign study has not yet been selected. The curriculum intentionally leaves this null instead of implying that an arbitrary secondary webpage is the assigned text.
- **The Falklands Campaign.** Lawrence Freedman’s _Official History of the Falklands Campaign_ is authoritative but copyrighted. UK National Archives and archived UK doctrine provide official supporting material, but no free official full-text campaign history has been selected.

## Instructor rule

Do not replace a null URL with a search result merely to make the link clickable. Prefer, in order: current official doctrine; official military/government history; declassified government primary source; public-domain archival edition; reputable scholarly work through a library. Record edition and access date when the exact text matters.
